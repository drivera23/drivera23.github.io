Informed Search for Regular Path Query Reachability

Diego Rivera Correa, Laurent Bindschaedler · 9th Joint Workshop on Graph Data Management Experiences & Systems and Network Data Analytics (GRADES-NDA), 2026

Studies heuristic-guided (greedy best-first / A*) search for evaluating Regular Path Query reachability over the product graph, finding that the search regime dominates heuristic choice and that DFS remains hard to beat for Boolean reachability.

Constructing 2D Watermarks by Composition

Alcibiades Bustillo, Diego Rivera Correa · SIDIM XXXVIII, 2023

This study presented a method of constructing 2D periodic arrays by composing a 1D periodic array. These arrays resulted in watermarks that were robust against many image-manipulating strategies.
